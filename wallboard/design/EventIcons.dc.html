Countdown icons · events and holidays

Eva sends a kind with each countdown; the tile uses that icon and colour, and the tap celebration uses that effect. Unknown kinds fall back to the star. Same line style as the rest of the board (24 grid, 1.9 stroke, round caps). Effects reuse three motions: burst/rain, rise, fall, with a different particle shape.
